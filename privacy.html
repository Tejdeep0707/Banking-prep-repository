This Privacy Policy applies to QuasiBanking Classes ("we", "us", "our") and governs how we handle personal information collected through our website at quasibanking.com and related services. By using our platform, you agree to the terms described below.
01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you register, enroll in a course, or contact us. We may also collect information automatically as you use our platform.

Personal Information
  • Full name and display name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (for enrollment and support)
  • City / State / Location (for regional batch matching)
Usage & Performance Data
  • Courses enrolled in and completion progress
  • Mock test scores, accuracy, and attempt history
  • Study streaks and daily activity logs
  • Pages visited and time spent on platform
  • Device type, browser, and IP address
Payment Information
  • Transaction reference IDs
  • Course purchased and amount paid
  • Payment method type (UPI, card, net banking)
Note: We do not store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials. All payments are processed by our third-party payment partner (Appx). Please refer to their privacy policy for payment data handling.
02

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve our platform and communicate with you. Specifically:

  • To create and manage your student account
  • To process course enrollments and provide access to purchased content
  • To display your progress, mock test scores, and performance analytics on your dashboard
  • To send you important updates: exam notifications, batch schedules, and current affairs
  • To respond to your support queries and enrollment inquiries
  • To send promotional offers via WhatsApp or email (you may opt out at any time)
  • To improve our content, mock tests, and user experience based on usage patterns
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.
03

Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with the following third-party services to deliver core functionality. Each has its own privacy policy and data practices:

Integrated Services
  • Appx Platform — Course delivery, video lessons, and mock test engine. When you click course or mock test links, you are redirected to Appx's platform and their privacy policy applies.
  • Firebase (Google) — User authentication, account management, and real-time database for your student dashboard.
  • WhatsApp Business — Optional communication channel for enrollment queries and support.
  • YouTube — Embedded free content and demo videos linked from our platform.
  • Google Analytics — Anonymous usage analytics to understand how students use the platform.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

04

Payment & Transactions

All course purchases and fee payments on QuasiBanking are processed through Appx, our authorized course delivery and payment partner. QuasiBanking does not directly process or store your payment card information.

We retain records of:

  • Which course was purchased and the transaction date
  • Transaction reference ID (for support purposes)
  • Enrollment status linked to your account

For refund requests, please refer to our Refund Policy or contact us directly.

05

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), which provides industry-standard security including encryption at rest and in transit.

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages and data transmissions
  • Firebase Authentication for secure login (email/password, Google Sign-In)
  • Role-based access controls — only authorized staff can access student data
  • Regular security reviews of our platform
While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users promptly as required by applicable law.
06

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember your theme preference (dark/light mode), and understand how you use our platform.

Types of Cookies We Use
  • Essential Cookies — Required for login sessions and core platform functionality. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies — Store your theme (dark/light) and display preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies — Help us understand usage patterns (via Google Analytics). These are anonymous and can be blocked via your browser settings.

You can disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. This will not affect core functionality.

Your Rights

As a user of QuasiBanking, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-Out — Unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time
  • Data Portability — Request your data in a structured, readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at Quasiclassee@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 7 business days.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at Quasiclassee@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.
