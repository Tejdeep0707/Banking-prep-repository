IBPS (PO / Clerk / RRB)

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Comprehensive Guide

Overview

IBPS exams are conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection for recruitment in public sector banks. These exams test aptitude, reasoning, English, and banking awareness.

Subjects Covered

  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • Reasoning Ability
  • English Language
  • General Awareness (Banking + Static GK)
  • Computer Knowledge (for Clerk/PO mains)

What You Get

Full syllabus coverage (Prelims + Mains)
Topic-wise video lessons
Daily practice quizzes
Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
Full-length mock tests with analysis

Best Strategy

  • Focus on speed + accuracy for prelims
  • Practice sectional mocks daily
  • Revise current affairs weekly
